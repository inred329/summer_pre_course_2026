> Notice: This document records a design review performed on 2026-08-03 against Constitution 1.2.0. Constitution 2.0 has replaced that version, so article numbers, fixed metadata, material IDs, AI records, and other mandatory conditions described here must not be treated as current policy. See [`reviews/repository-constitution-2-audit.en.md`](../reviews/repository-constitution-2-audit.en.md) for the current repository-wide audit.

## Document Purpose

This file preserves the background, judgments, and historical decisions of the earlier review so maintainers can understand how the initial design documents were formed.

It is not a summary of Constitution 2.0, must not create new rules, and must not serve as the current acceptance checklist for student materials, instructor documents, or assessments.

## Original Review Scope

- Chinese and English Course Constitution, version 1.2.0.
- `design/01-product-vision.*` through `design/11-terminology-glossary.*`.
- The root `README.md` and bilingual design indexes as they existed at that time.

## Summary of the Original Conclusion

The earlier review concluded that the design baseline could proceed to material and activity development and contributed to:

- A Chinese–English terminology glossary.
- Traceability and version records for design documents.
- Connections among competencies, acceptance, risks, and traceability.
- Stronger attention to code, testing, and bilingual consistency.

These historical outcomes may remain useful, but they must be reconsidered under Constitution 2.0. A former compliant judgment does not establish current compliance.

## Requirements That Have Expired or Require Reassessment

The following former requirements no longer have universal mandatory force:

- Every material must cite requirement and competency IDs.
- Every material must display target maturity, scope state, and internal metadata.
- Every activity must state AI boundaries and retain AI-use records.
- Every student material must use a fixed section structure and fixed information-level labels.
- Former constitutional article numbers may be used directly as current acceptance criteria.

When any of these ideas remains useful, it must be redefined by an appropriate Standard, Guide, or official Policy and must not pollute the main student reading flow.

## Permitted Historical Use

Maintainers may use this document to:

- Understand early governance decisions.
- Trace the origin of selected design documents.
- Compare governance under Constitution 1.2.0 and 2.0.

Maintainers must not use this document to:

- Declare current files compliant with Constitution 2.0.
- Create new official policy.
- Require students or instructors to follow expired AI, metadata, or ID rules.
- Replace the current repository audit or authoritative sources.
